# outputs

Every finished video and image the pipeline produces, in one place.

Working files stay where they are generated (`agent_b/real_samples/output/`, which is
gitignored and holds thousands of per-frame PNGs and raw depth arrays). This folder holds only
the things worth looking at or sending to someone.

## What is in here

| folder | contents |
|---|---|
| `control/` | The instruction tracks: distance maps, edge maps, clay renders. What the AI was told to draw. |
| `generated/` | The finished photoreal videos, one per generator run. |
| `plans/` | Top-down floor plans with the camera route drawn on, for checking a route before spending on a render. |

## Naming

    <source>_<subject>_<what>[_<variant>].<ext>

    pdf_apartment_clay.mp4              control track, built from the PDF floor plan
    pdf_apartment_ltx3dreal_strong.mp4  generated, LTX 2.3 3DREAL at strong intensity

`source` is `pdf` or `dwg`, so it is always clear which route produced a file. That matters:
the two are different pipelines with different accuracy, and a video with no provenance is a
video nobody can defend.

## Why the media is not committed

`.gitignore` excludes the video and image files. They are large, they are regenerated by
running the scripts, and a repository is a poor place to store binaries that change often.

`MANIFEST.md` **is** committed, and records what was generated, from what, with which settings
and what it measured. That way the history survives even though the files do not, and a result
can be reproduced rather than merely remembered.
